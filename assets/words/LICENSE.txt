common-en.txt: the 40,000 most frequent English words, exported from the wordfreq project
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, data by Robyn Speer et al.).

wordfreq's word-frequency data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This derived list is shared under the same licence.

Tiro uses it only to recognise ordinary words, so its sound-alike correction never rewrites them.

cmudict.txt.gz: pronunciations from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict),
re-encoded by tools/make_cmudict.py (one character per phone, stress dropped). Tiro uses it to tell whether two
spellings sound alike. Its licence:
